◦ Part 1 of 2 — Intake

15 minutes of homework.
Then we take it from here.

Answer directly in an email to taiga.gamell@gmail.com with subject line: Taiga intake — [Your Business Name]. Take 15 minutes, be specific where it matters — your own words beat polished answers every time.

The business
Q 01

Business name exactly as it should appear everywhere.

Legal name + display name if different. This becomes your NAP standard across all platforms.

Q 02

Address, phone, and email — exactly as they should appear online.

Consistency here directly affects local search ranking. One format, used everywhere.

Q 03

Services or classes offered, and the 1–3 you most want to grow.

Cash services first. We'll build dedicated pages for the top earners.

Q 04

Which prices are you comfortable publishing publicly?

Yes or no per service. Publishing prices reduces low-intent contacts and improves SEO.

Q 05

Who is your ideal customer?

Describe your favorite current patient or member — age, situation, what they were dealing with before, why they're a great fit.

Q 06

Name your top 3 local competitors. What annoys you about their marketing?

We'll benchmark against them and find where you can clearly own territory they're ignoring.

The goal
Q 07

What does a great month look like in numbers?

New patients, members, inbound calls, trial signups — whatever the metric is, put a number on it.

Q 08

What's the single action a website visitor should take?

Book, call, or trial signup. One CTA per page. We need to know which one drives your business.

Q 09

Booking system in use — link and how we access it.

Mindbody, Jane, Calendly, Square, custom — include the URL. Credentials come via password manager, not here.

Brand & taste
Q 10

Three websites you like — any industry — and one word about each.

Not necessarily competitors. Just sites that feel right to you. Your gut reaction tells us more than any brief.

Q 11

Anything about your current brand you want to keep.

Logo, colors, tagline, a specific photo. Tell us what's sacred and what's up for grabs.

Q 12

Words you'd use to describe your practice or gym. Words you'd never use.

Both lists matter equally. The "never" list shapes voice faster than anything.

Q 13

Do you have photos of the space, team, and clients (with consent)?

Real photos outperform stock by a wide margin. If none exist, we'll schedule a shoot — it's worth it.

Proof
Q 19

Paste your 3 best reviews or testimonials.

Google, Yelp, email, direct message — wherever they live. Verbatim is best.

Q 20

Any before/afters, transformations, or results you're proud of.

With consent to publish. This becomes social proof on the site and a source for GBP posts.

◦ What we're building toward

Proof we look for and the kind we help you collect.

The best proof is specific. Vague praise doesn't convert. Real results do.

What makes strong proof

Specificity converts. Generic doesn't.

"Great service" is noise. "Went from 8 patients a day to 14 in the first 90 days" is signal. Before/after numbers, named results, and specific outcomes are what turn a site visitor into a booked appointment.

What we do with your proof

We place it where it earns its keep.

Testimonials on service pages, results in FAQs, transformation photos as GBP posts. Nothing goes in a sidebar. Every proof element has a conversion job and a search optimization angle.
