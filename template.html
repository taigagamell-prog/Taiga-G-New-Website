◦ Questions

Before you buy.

Do I need to know how to code?+

For basic setup — swapping text, photos, and contact info — no. Framer lets you edit visually, the way you'd edit a Canva or Google Doc. If you want to customize beyond the template's design (layouts, custom interactions), that may require a developer. Most owners hand the Framer link to their web person and are live in an afternoon.

What if I don't want to use Framer?+

Raw HTML/CSS files are also included. You can host those on Vercel, Netlify, or anywhere that accepts static files — free tiers exist on both. The HTML version requires editing files directly, so a basic comfort with code (or a developer) helps. Framer is the recommended path for non-coders.

What does Framer hosting cost?+

Framer's site hosting runs $10–25/month depending on the plan. That's separate from the $500 template purchase. The raw HTML version can be hosted free on Vercel's hobby plan. Either way, the $500 is a one-time cost — no ongoing payments to Taiga.

Will this actually help me rank in search?+

Structure is one part of ranking — content, citations, and GBP activity are the rest. The Taiga System gives you the foundation that makes all of that compound: correct schema, fast load time, FAQ structure for AI citation, and the geo-content formula to keep adding pages over time. The structure won't hold you back. Most sites' structure does.

The Taiga System is the bones — template, schema, and playbooks. You supply the brand, the words, and the ongoing work. That's the honest difference.
