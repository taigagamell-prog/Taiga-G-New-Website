◦ The agreement — plain language
Parties & scope.

Taiga ("we") will deliver the Rebuild scope above for your business ("you"). Anything outside that scope is quoted separately before work begins — no surprise invoices.

Payment.

50% deposit ($1,500) to begin; remaining 50% ($1,500) invoiced at launch, due within 7 days. The monthly plan starts at launch and is billed monthly in advance.

Timeline & your part.

~4 weeks from the day we receive your completed intake and credentials. Delays in providing content, access, or approvals pause the clock, not the quality.

Revisions.

One structured revision round on brand direction and one on the site before launch. After launch, changes flow through your monthly plan.

Ownership.

On final payment, you own the website, brand assets, and content we created for you, fully. We may show the work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Monthly plan & credits.

Month-to-month, cancel anytime with 30 days' written notice. Each plan runs on credits — one credit = one geo blog post or one revision. Unused credits roll over one month only; they don't stack beyond that. If a 12-month Growth commitment was applied to discount this Rebuild and you cancel early, the discounted amount becomes due.

Optional path
Independence handoff.

If at launch you'd rather take over the site and SEO in-house instead of staying on retainer, that's a legitimate choice. Taiga will document every build decision, transfer all access and credentials, and run a live training session covering the tools, workflows, and monthly rhythm. Fee is negotiated based on scope — typically $500–$2,500. Fair heads up: the setup, keyword logic, and ongoing cadence are more involved than they look from the outside. Most clients who price this out find the retainer is the better math. But the keys are always yours if you want them.

Cancellation of the Rebuild.

Before work starts: full deposit refund. After work starts: the deposit covers work performed and is non-refundable; you keep everything produced to date.

Good faith & limits.

We optimize aggressively for rankings and results but no one can guarantee a specific Google position — anyone who does is lying to you. Our liability is capped at fees paid. Montana law governs.
