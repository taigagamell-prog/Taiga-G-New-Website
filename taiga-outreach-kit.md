## 5. The audit call — run it in 20 minutes

1. **0–2 min · Frame it.** "This is your audit, not my pitch. I'll show you three things live, you keep everything either way."
2. **2–8 min · Search, live.** Google "[service] near me" and "[service] [city]" on screen share. Show map pack position. Ask ChatGPT for a local rec. Let the absence speak.
3. **8–14 min · The site, live.** Load their site on mobile. Time it out loud. Try to book an appointment. Narrate the friction.
4. **14–18 min · The three things.** Name them plainly (from the audit template). Attach a cost to each: "every month this leak stays open is [N] patients."
5. **18–20 min · The path.** "Two ways forward: the Rebuild fixes all three at $6,500, or take the audit and fix it yourself — genuinely fine. If you commit to 12 months of Growth, the Rebuild is half off."

**Never:** discount the monthly, pitch before the search demo, or run past 25 minutes.

## 7. Weekly outreach rhythm (solo-founder sustainable)

- **Mon:** build list of 25 (one vertical, one city). Check each: map pack position, site speed, GBP last post.
- **Tue–Wed:** send 25 cold emails + 10 LinkedIn connects. Personalize sentence one only.
- **Thu:** follow-ups due today. Run any booked audits.
- **Fri:** send breakdowns to responders, post one piece of content (a before/after, a terrain report screenshot).

25 sends/week at realistic local reply rates ≈ 2–4 audit calls/week. Close 1 in 3 audits → 3–4 Rebuilds a month. That's the machine.
